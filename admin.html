General Instructions

  1. Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.
  2. Each correct answer will receive the specified positive marks.
  3. A wrong answer will attract the specified negative marking.
  4. An unanswered question will receive 0 marks.
  5. The test will be automatically submitted when the allotted time expires.
  6. Once the test is submitted, it cannot be resumed or edited.
  7. Do not refresh, close, or navigate away from the test window while the test is in progress.
  8. Make sure you have a stable internet connection before starting.
  9. Use the available time wisely and review your answers before final submission whenever possible.
  10. Your result and answer review will be available after submission.
